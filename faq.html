Yes, but you have to act fast! Please email us within 1 hour of placing your order, and we will do our best to update or cancel it before it gets processed.

HOW DO I CONTACT CUSTOMER SUPPORT?

The fastest way is through our Live Chat at the bottom of the screen. You can also email us anytime, and we usually reply within 24 hours.

As of now, we only ship within the Kingdom of Cambodia. We do not accept, nor do we provide international shipping.
